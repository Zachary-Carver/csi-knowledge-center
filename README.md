## Scope

Crime scene, trauma and biohazard, unattended death and decomposition, hoarding, odor-source, vehicle biohazard, and extreme-mess cleanup in Denton, DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. CSI is a mobile service-area business and does not publish a customer-facing street address.
