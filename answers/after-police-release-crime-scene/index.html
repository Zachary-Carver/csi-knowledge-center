What to Do After Police Release a Crime Scene

Once police release a scene, restrict access, identify the authorized property decision-maker, document only what is necessary, and contact a specialty cleanup provider.

Law enforcement investigates a crime scene but generally does not perform property cleanup. Avoid touching blood, bodily fluids, damaged porous materials, or items in the affected area. Do not move affected belongings through clean rooms. Confirm who has authority for the property, note insurer instructions when applicable, and arrange an assessment.

For help with a specific scene: Call CSI at 940-654-6334 or visit the relevant official service page.

Important limitations

This article is general educational information. Scene conditions vary. For immediate danger, an active crime, or a medical emergency, call 911.
