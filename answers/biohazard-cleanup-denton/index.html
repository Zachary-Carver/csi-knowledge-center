Biohazard cleanup in Denton, Texas

CSI: Clean Scene Investigators provides 24/7 specialty biohazard cleanup in Denton and Denton County for crime scenes, trauma, blood and bodily fluids, unattended deaths, decomposition, hazardous hoarding conditions, forensic odor, and vehicle contamination.

What counts as specialty biohazard cleanup?

Biohazard remediation addresses biological contamination and related hazards that go beyond routine cleaning. Depending on the event, this can include contaminated porous materials, hidden migration, odor sources, affected contents, vehicle interiors, and areas impacted by blood or bodily fluids.

Crime scene and trauma response

When law enforcement is involved, cleanup begins only after the scene has been released. CSI's role is remediation and restoration-related specialty cleanup, not investigation or evidence handling.

Unattended death and decomposition

Decomposition can affect materials beyond the immediately visible area and create persistent odor when source contamination remains. CSI evaluates the affected materials and addresses contamination and odor at the source as appropriate to the scene.
