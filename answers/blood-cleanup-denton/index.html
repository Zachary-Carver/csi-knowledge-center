Professional blood cleanup in Denton, Texas

Blood and bodily-fluid contamination can extend beyond what is visible, particularly when it reaches carpet, padding, upholstery, seams, flooring, subflooring, walls, contents, or vehicle components. CSI: Clean Scene Investigators provides specialty blood cleanup throughout Denton and Denton County.

Why ordinary cleaning may not be enough

Surface appearance alone does not show whether biological material migrated into porous or concealed materials. Effective remediation depends on identifying the affected area, selecting appropriate methods for the materials involved, and removing or remediating contamination rather than simply making the surface look clean.

Scenes CSI handles

Blood cleanup may be part of a crime scene, traumatic injury, suicide or homicide scene, unattended death, decomposition scene, vehicle incident, or other biological contamination event. CSI's work is limited to specialty cleanup and remediation after the property or vehicle has been released by the appropriate authority when law enforcement is involved.
