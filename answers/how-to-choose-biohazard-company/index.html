Hiring checklist

How do you choose a crime scene or biohazard cleanup company?

Choose based on scope clarity, specialty experience, documentation, contamination assessment and professional communication, not only the lowest number quoted before anyone understands the scene.

Questions worth asking

What exactly is included in the quote?

Ask for the affected areas, cleaning or removal tasks, disposal, odor treatment and any exclusions.

What would cause the estimate to change?

The company should be able to explain what hidden findings or additional contamination would expand the scope.

How are porous materials evaluated?

Ask how carpet, padding, flooring, drywall, upholstery or subfloor are assessed when fluids may have penetrated.

How is waste handled?

Ask how regulated or contaminated waste generated by the work is packaged, transported and disposed of as applicable.

How is privacy protected?

Professional communication, discreet arrival and careful handling of scene information matter as much as technical cleanup.
