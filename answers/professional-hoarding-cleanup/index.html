How Professional Hoarding Cleanup Works

A respectful hoarding cleanup begins with authorization, goals, hazards, priorities, and a realistic scope—not indiscriminate disposal.

Projects may be phased by room, safety concern, property deadline, or available decision-making support. Items are evaluated according to authorization, condition, contamination, and project goals. Some properties contain additional hazards that change the safety plan.

For help with a specific scene: Call CSI at 940-654-6334 or visit the relevant official service page.

Important limitations

This article is general educational information. Scene conditions vary. For immediate danger, an active crime, or a medical emergency, call 911.
