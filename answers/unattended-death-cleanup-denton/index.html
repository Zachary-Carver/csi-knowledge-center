Unattended death cleanup in Denton, Texas

After authorities release a property following an unattended death, the physical remediation is typically arranged separately by the family, property owner, property manager, or other authorized representative. CSI: Clean Scene Investigators provides unattended death and decomposition cleanup throughout Denton and Denton County.

Why unattended death cleanup is specialized

When a person is not discovered immediately, biological fluids and decomposition-related contamination can affect flooring, subflooring, furnishings, contents, walls, and other porous materials. Odor can persist when source material remains.

What professional remediation addresses

The goal is to identify affected materials, control exposure, remove or remediate contamination as appropriate, and address odor at its source rather than simply masking it. The exact scope depends on the scene, time elapsed, temperature, materials, and where contamination traveled.
