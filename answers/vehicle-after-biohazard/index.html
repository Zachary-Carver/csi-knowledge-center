Can a Vehicle Be Restored After a Biohazard Incident?

Sometimes—but salvageability depends on where material traveled, the affected components, access, duration, and the vehicle’s condition.

Vehicle interiors contain seams, foam, carpet, padding, tracks, trim, vents, wiring areas, and enclosed cavities. A visible stain may not show the full affected area. Avoid driving the vehicle when occupants could contact affected material.

Important limitations

This article is general educational information. Scene conditions vary. For immediate danger, an active crime, or a medical emergency, call 911.
