Who Cleans a Home After an Unattended Death in DFW?

After authorities release the property, a specialty biohazard cleanup company handles the physical cleanup—not police, the medical examiner, or a funeral home.

An unattended death can affect more than the immediately visible area. Fluids and odor compounds may reach flooring layers, porous furnishings, seams, walls, or nearby spaces. A specialty assessment identifies affected materials, establishes work-area controls, and determines what may be cleaned, treated, or removed. Families should keep people and pets away from the area and avoid moving belongings until the scene is released and a safe retrieval plan is established.

For help with a specific scene: Call CSI at 940-654-6334 or visit the relevant official service page.

Important limitations

This article is general educational information. Scene conditions vary. For immediate danger, an active crime, or a medical emergency, call 911.
