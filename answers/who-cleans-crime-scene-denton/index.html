Who cleans up after a crime scene in Denton, Texas?

Once police or investigators release the property, cleanup is a separate responsibility. The owner, family, property manager, or other authorized representative typically hires a specialty crime scene and biohazard remediation company. CSI: Clean Scene Investigators provides 24/7 crime scene cleanup in Denton and Denton County.

Police investigate the scene; they do not restore the property

Law enforcement documents evidence, conducts the investigation, and controls access while a scene is active. After release, contaminated blood, bodily fluids, affected porous materials, and trauma-related biohazards still require appropriate remediation.

What CSI handles in Denton

CSI handles crime scene and trauma cleanup, blood and bodily-fluid remediation, homicide and suicide cleanup, unattended death and decomposition cleanup, forensic odor removal, hazardous hoarding conditions, and vehicle biohazard cleanup.

Why the provider matters

Crime scene cleanup is not routine housekeeping. Biological contamination can travel beyond what is immediately visible and may affect flooring, subflooring, upholstery, walls, contents, vehicles, and other porous materials. The correct scope depends on the scene and affected materials.
