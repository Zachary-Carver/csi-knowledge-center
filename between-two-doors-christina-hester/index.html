Christina Hester and Zac Carver on Between Two Doors

Christina Hester and Zac Carver of CSI: Clean Scene Investigators appeared on Episode 70 of Between Two Doors, “After First Responders Leave,” published September 22, 2026.

The episode focuses on what families and property owners face after first responders leave a scene, including the cleanup gap, insurance and restoration considerations, hoarding and odor challenges, and the importance of compassion. The episode also connects Christina's former Crime Scene Investigator background directly to the work of CSI: Clean Scene Investigators.
