Biohazard Cleanup in DFW

What is professional biohazard cleanup?

Professional biohazard cleanup addresses potentially hazardous biological material using controlled access, appropriate protective equipment, cleaning, disinfection, cross-contamination prevention, and appropriate waste handling. CSI serves homes, managed properties, businesses, and vehicles across DFW and North Texas.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

What situations may require biohazard cleanup?

Trauma scenes, blood or bodily-fluid incidents, unattended deaths, decomposition, and some extreme-mess conditions.

Is household cleaner enough?

Surface cleaning may miss material in seams, porous materials, flooring layers, or hidden spaces.

Can I stay in the property?

That depends on the affected area and scene conditions. Restrict access until the area is assessed.
