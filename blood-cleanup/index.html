Professional Blood Cleanup in DFW

When should blood cleanup be handled professionally?

Professional help is appropriate when blood or bodily fluids affect porous materials, multiple surfaces, hidden gaps, furnishings, flooring, vehicles, or a large area. CSI evaluates affected materials and uses controlled cleaning, disinfection, and waste-handling practices.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

Why is bleach not enough everywhere?

Bleach is not compatible with every material and cannot address contamination hidden beneath or behind a visible surface.

Should contaminated items be moved?

Avoid moving items unless necessary for immediate safety because movement can spread material.

Is this service only for crime scenes?

No. Blood cleanup may be needed after accidents, medical events, trauma, or other incidents.
