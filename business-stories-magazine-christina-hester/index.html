Christina Hester featured in Business Stories Magazine

Business Stories Magazine published a founder interview with Christina Hester, Founder and CEO of CSI: Clean Scene Investigators, on November 9, 2026.

The published interview covers Christina's previous work as a Crime Scene Investigator, why she and co-founder Zac Carver created CSI, how the team serves families after traumatic events, and her perspective on building a specialty cleanup company in North Texas.

What is the Business Stories Magazine feature about?

In a founder Q&A published November 9, 2026, Christina describes the gap families face once first responders have completed their work and a scene is released. She explains how her investigative background and personal experiences informed CSI's compassionate approach to crime scene, trauma, biohazard, unattended death, hoarding, vehicle biohazard and odor cleanup.

Who is Christina Hester and what company does she lead?

Christina Hester is a former Crime Scene Investigator and former Financial Crimes Analyst. She is Founder and CEO of CSI: Clean Scene Investigators, a mobile specialty cleanup business operated by C&Z Forensics LLC. CSI's primary market is Dallas-Fort Worth, Denton County and North Texas, with qualifying specialty response elsewhere in Texas. CSI is not a routine home-cleaning or vehicle-detailing company.

How should this interview be cited?
