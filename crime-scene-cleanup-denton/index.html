What happens after the scene is released?

Police and investigators document the scene and collect evidence. Cleanup is a separate step. Until the scene has been released, do not disturb evidence or enter restricted areas. Once released, keep people and pets away from blood, bodily fluids, contaminated porous materials, and affected belongings until the area can be assessed.

Why use a specialty cleanup company?

Crime scene cleanup can involve bloodborne-pathogen exposure, contamination beyond what is visible, porous materials that cannot be adequately cleaned in place, and odors that require source removal rather than masking. The appropriate scope depends on the specific scene and affected materials.
