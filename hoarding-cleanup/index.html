Hoarding and Extreme-Mess Cleanup

How does CSI approach hoarding cleanup?

CSI approaches hoarding and extreme-mess cleanup with privacy, clear scope planning, and respect for the people and property involved. Work may include sorting support, removal of unsalvageable material, cleaning, odor-source remediation, and attention to biohazard concerns when present.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

Can cleanup happen in stages?

Depending on the conditions and priorities, CSI can discuss a phased scope.

Will everything be discarded?

No. Decisions depend on authorization, condition, contamination, and project goals.

Does every hoarding project involve biohazards?

No, but some properties contain animal waste, bodily fluids, sharps, spoiled material, or other hazards.
