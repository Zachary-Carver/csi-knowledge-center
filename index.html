Who cleans up after a crime scene in Texas?

After law enforcement releases the property, the owner, family, property manager or authorized representative generally arranges specialty cleanup. CSI: Clean Scene Investigators provides crime scene, trauma, blood, unattended death, decomposition and biohazard cleanup statewide throughout Texas.

What should you do first?

For immediate danger, an active crime or a medical emergency, call 911. Do not enter an active or unreleased scene. Keep people and pets away from affected areas and avoid touching or moving contaminated materials.
