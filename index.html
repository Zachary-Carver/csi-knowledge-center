Who is CSI?

CSI: Clean Scene Investigators is a mobile specialty cleanup company led by former Crime Scene Investigator Christina Hester. CSI serves families, property professionals, businesses, and referral partners across DFW, Denton County, and North Texas with forensic precision and trauma-informed compassion.
This is CSI’s educational knowledge center. For official service details, availability, or help with a scene, visit www.cleansceneinvestigators.com or call 940-654-6334.

What should you do first?

For immediate danger, an active crime, or a medical emergency, call 911. Do not enter an active or unreleased scene. Keep people and pets away from affected areas and avoid touching or moving contaminated materials.
