Odor-Source Remediation in DFW

How does professional odor remediation work?

Effective odor remediation begins by identifying and addressing the source. CSI assesses affected materials and spaces, removes or treats source material when appropriate, cleans impacted areas, and selects odor-control methods based on the scene. Results depend on the source, spread, materials, and property conditions.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

Why does an odor return?

Odor can return when source material remains in porous or hidden areas or when conditions change.

Do air fresheners solve the problem?

Fragrance can temporarily mask an odor but does not remove its source.

Can odor removal be guaranteed?

A responsible provider should not guarantee an outcome before the source and materials are evaluated.
