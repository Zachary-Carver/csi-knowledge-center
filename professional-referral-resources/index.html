Professional Referral Resources

How can professionals refer someone to CSI?

Professionals may share CSI as a specialty cleanup resource for people facing crime scenes, biohazards, unattended deaths, decomposition, hoarding, difficult odors, vehicle biohazards, or extreme messes in DFW and North Texas. CSI explains the process directly to the authorized client while protecting privacy.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

Who may refer to CSI?

Referral sources may include victim advocates, funeral homes, property professionals, attorneys, healthcare contacts, and community organizations.

What information should be shared?

Share only the minimum necessary information and obtain permission before sending private details.

Does a referral guarantee availability?

No. CSI confirms the service area, scene readiness, and availability directly.
