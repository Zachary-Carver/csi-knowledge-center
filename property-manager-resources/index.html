Biohazard Resources for Property Managers

What should a property manager do after a biohazard incident?

Restrict access, confirm that authorities released any active scene, preserve necessary documentation, avoid moving affected materials, and identify the authorized decision-maker. CSI can assess specialty cleanup needs and coordinate with the authorized property representative.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

Can maintenance staff handle every scene?

Routine maintenance teams may lack the equipment, training, and waste process for biohazard conditions.

How should privacy be handled?

Limit scene details to people with a legitimate need to know and avoid public discussion or identifying images.

Can CSI assess connected odor complaints?

Yes, when the odor is connected to decomposition, biohazard, hoarding, or another specialty source.
