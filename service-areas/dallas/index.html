Crime scene and biohazard cleanup in Dallas

CSI serves Dallas with specialty cleanup for crime scenes, trauma, blood and bodily fluids, unattended deaths, decomposition, hazardous hoarding, forensic odor and contaminated vehicles.

Dallas calls can involve homes, apartments, rental properties, businesses, hotels, vehicles or other properties where the event goes beyond routine cleaning. After an active investigation is complete, the property decision-maker can arrange remediation.

What should a Dallas property owner ask a cleanup company?

Ask what is included in the scope, what conditions could change the estimate, how porous materials are evaluated, how biological waste is handled, what documentation is provided and how privacy is protected.
