Blood and biohazard cleanup in Garland

CSI serves Garland and the Dallas area for specialty blood cleanup, crime scenes, trauma, unattended death, decomposition, hazardous hoarding, forensic odor and contaminated vehicles.

When blood is present on hardwood or another finished surface, the visible stain is only one part of the assessment. Seams, transitions, baseboards and underlying materials may also matter depending on how much fluid was present and how long it remained.
