Where does CSI: Clean Scene Investigators provide specialty cleanup?

Direct answer: CSI is based in North Texas with Denton, Denton County and the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex as its home market. The company also provides statewide Texas response for qualifying specialty cleanup calls.

Denton County and North Texas

Denton is a priority local market. Named nearby communities include Lewisville, Flower Mound, Frisco, Little Elm, Argyle, Aubrey, Ponder, Krum, Sanger, Corinth, Lake Dallas and Cross Roads. Service availability depends on the type of incident, timing and travel requirements.

Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex

CSI serves DFW communities including Dallas, Fort Worth, McKinney, Plano, Arlington, Irving, Carrollton, Grapevine and Garland, with additional metro-area response available.

Statewide Texas response

CSI publicly identifies Texas as its broader service territory. Calls outside North Texas are evaluated based on the specialty need, response logistics and availability. The business remains one mobile service-area company, not a chain of separate city offices.
