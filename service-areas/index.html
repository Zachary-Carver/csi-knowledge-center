Statewide Texas response

The city-page list is not the outer boundary of service

CSI publicly serves qualifying specialty cleanup calls throughout Texas. That broader service territory includes communities outside the 26 current local landing pages, including major markets such as Austin, Houston, San Antonio, El Paso, Waco, Tyler, Amarillo, Lubbock, Midland, Odessa and Corpus Christi, plus smaller Texas communities where response logistics allow.
