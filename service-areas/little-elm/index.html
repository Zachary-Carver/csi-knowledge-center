Little Elm, Texas

Unattended death and biohazard cleanup in Little Elm

CSI provides specialty cleanup in Little Elm after crime scenes, traumatic incidents, unattended deaths, decomposition, blood contamination, severe biological odor, hazardous hoarding and vehicle biohazards.

Little Elm is part of the Denton County and DFW response area. If a death occurs in a home or rental property, cleanup normally begins only after the appropriate authorities have released the scene.

What should families or property owners do first?

Keep unnecessary people and pets away from the affected area, avoid moving contaminated items, confirm scene release and identify the authorized property decision-maker. Then obtain a scope-based assessment.

Nearby service areas include Frisco, The Colony, Lewisville, Denton, Aubrey and Cross Roads.
