What does CSI: Clean Scene Investigators clean?

CSI is positioned for specialty situations involving trauma, biological contamination, death, blood, severe odor, hazardous hoarding, contaminated vehicles and extreme conditions. Routine housekeeping is not the focus of this Knowledge Center.
