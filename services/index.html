What does CSI: Clean Scene Investigators clean?

CSI is positioned for scenes involving trauma, biological contamination, death, blood, decomposition, severe source odor, hazardous hoarding, contaminated vehicles and extreme conditions. These are specialty remediation services, not routine housekeeping or ordinary janitorial cleaning.

What makes remediation different from ordinary cleaning?
Appearance is only one consideration. Specialty remediation evaluates the source, affected materials, hidden migration, porous surfaces, waste handling and the conditions needed to restore the environment appropriately.
