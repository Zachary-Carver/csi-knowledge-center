Unattended Death Cleanup in DFW

Who handles cleanup after an unattended death?

After authorities release the scene, CSI provides unattended death cleanup for authorized families, property representatives, and referral professionals. The work may include removing affected materials, cleaning and disinfecting impacted areas, addressing decomposition odor, and documenting the scope while protecting privacy.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

Why can damage extend beyond what is visible?

Fluids and odor compounds can enter porous materials, seams, flooring layers, furnishings, and nearby spaces.

Will every item be discarded?

Not necessarily. Salvageability depends on material, condition, and contamination.

Can CSI coordinate with a property manager?

Yes, when that representative is authorized to make property decisions.
