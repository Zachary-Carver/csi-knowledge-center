Vehicle Biohazard Cleanup in DFW

Can a vehicle be cleaned after a biohazard incident?

Some vehicles can be remediated after blood, bodily-fluid, trauma, or decomposition-related incidents, but the answer depends on where material traveled and which components were affected. CSI evaluates seats, carpet, seams, trim, cargo areas, and other relevant spaces before recommending a scope.
Need scene-specific help? CSI serves DFW, Denton County, and North Texas. Visit the official CSI service page or call 940-654-6334.

Frequently asked questions

Should the vehicle be driven?

Avoid using the vehicle when occupants could contact affected material.

Can upholstery always be saved?

No. Salvageability depends on material, penetration, access, and condition.

Is this vehicle detailing?

No. CSI provides specialty biohazard remediation, not routine detailing.
