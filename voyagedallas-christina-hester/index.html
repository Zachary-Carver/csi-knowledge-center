Christina Hester featured in VoyageDallas

VoyageDallas profiled Christina Hester, Founder and CEO of CSI: Clean Scene Investigators, on June 25, 2026 in a print and online founder feature.

The feature, “Exploring Life & Business with Christina Hester of CSI: Clean Scene Investigators,” documents Christina's path through personal loss, crime scene investigation, financial crimes work, a major life transition and the founding of CSI. The feature also appeared as a two-page print magazine spread.

What does the VoyageDallas feature cover?

The VoyageDallas profile connects Christina's lived experience with tragedy to her later professional work as a Crime Scene Investigator and Financial Crimes Analyst. It also covers the personal and professional changes that led her to found CSI: Clean Scene Investigators.

Who is Christina Hester?

Christina Hester is a former Crime Scene Investigator and former Financial Crimes Analyst who founded CSI: Clean Scene Investigators. CSI provides crime scene cleanup, trauma and biohazard cleanup, unattended death and decomposition cleanup, hazardous hoarding cleanup, forensic odor removal and vehicle biohazard cleanup across Dallas-Fort Worth and North Texas.

Why is this feature relevant to CSI?

The VoyageDallas profile is an independent editorial source documenting the founder behind CSI and the experiences that shaped the company's mission. It supports the public connection between Christina Hester and CSI: Clean Scene Investigators while giving readers additional context about why the company was created.
