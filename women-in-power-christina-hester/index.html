About Christina Hester

Christina Hester is a former Crime Scene Investigator, former Financial Crimes Analyst, public speaker, podcast host and entrepreneur. She founded CSI: Clean Scene Investigators to bring investigative discipline, forensic precision and trauma-informed compassion to crime scene, trauma, biohazard, blood, unattended death, decomposition, hazardous hoarding, forensic odor and vehicle biohazard cleanup.
